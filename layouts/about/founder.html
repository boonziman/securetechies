The person who built the firm

Alex has more than twenty years in enterprise IT infrastructure, managed services, cybersecurity, cloud, and technology consulting. He has designed and supported environments for small and midsize businesses and for larger organizations that had outgrown a single in-house generalist.

The stack is the one Southern California firms actually run: Microsoft 365 and Azure, VMware, HPE infrastructure and dHCI, Cisco UCS, enterprise storage, backup and disaster recovery, cybersecurity, endpoint management, networking, virtual desktops, and hybrid cloud. He does not treat those as logo slides. They are the rooms he has had to keep up.

Before Secure Techies took all of his time, Alex worked as an IT infrastructure and solutions architect. The projects looked familiar: modernize aging hardware, close security gaps, cut downtime, and stop buying the wrong gear. That is still the work. The difference is he now owns the relationship after the install.

He works with organizations across Southern California and farther out. The offer is managed IT, security, cloud, Microsoft 365, recovery, infrastructure consulting, technology procurement, and the kind of strategic IT leadership a mid-size firm cannot hire as one employee. You get that judgment without carrying the payroll of an internal enterprise department.
