A local IT department, not a black box

Secure Techies, LLC started in 2016 and still works out of Canoga Park. The work has not changed much in spirit: keep offices running, keep client data safe, and answer the phone when something breaks.

About half of our clients are law firms. The rest include healthcare practices, professional services, construction and job sites, finance, and growing companies that need serious IT without hiring a full internal staff. What they share is simple. Downtime is expensive. Ticket queues that never learn the environment are worse.

We monitor systems, handle day-to-day support, harden security, plan upgrades, and come on site when remote work is not enough. Staff stay on client work instead of troubleshooting printers and phishing emails.

We treat every client environment like it is our own. That has been the rule since 2016.
