Who we are

Managed IT without the black box

Secure Techies, LLC is based at 21515 Vanowen St, Suite 1110 in Canoga Park. We work as the outsourced IT department for firms that need dependable support, real security, and a partner who understands how Southern California offices actually run.

Roughly half of our clients are law firms. The rest span healthcare, professional services, construction and job sites, finance, and growing SMBs. What they share is simple: technology has to work, downtime is expensive, and ticket queues without ownership are not acceptable.

We monitor systems, fix day-to-day issues, harden security, plan upgrades, and show up on site when remote support is not enough — so your staff can stay on client work instead of troubleshooting printers and phishing emails.
