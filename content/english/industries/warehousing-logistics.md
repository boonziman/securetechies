## IT support for warehouses and logistics operators

Secure Techies provides **warehousing and logistics IT** for Southern California operators who cannot miss a pickup window. From Canoga Park we run [managed help desk](/services/managed-help-desk/), [infrastructure](/services/infrastructure/), and [mobile CCTV trailers](/services/mobile-cctv-trailers/) as one plan — office, docks, and yard.

A warehouse is two businesses sharing a building. Headquarters has accounting and a WMS login. The dock has scanners, a weak access point, and a yard that goes dark after the last truck. Most MSPs only staff the first business. A worked yard project is [a mobile surveillance trailer on a warehouse yard](/case-studies/warehouse-mobile-surveillance-trailer/).

### Who we work with

Independent warehouses, 3PLs, and distribution operators with a Southern California building — often one to a few sites. Plants that need an IT/OT boundary start at [manufacturing](/industries/manufacturing/). Contractors with trailers start at [construction](/industries/construction/).

### What is included

A standard engagement covers:

- [Help desk](/services/managed-help-desk/) for shipping, inventory, and accounting
- WMS environment — identity, devices, network. Application support stays with the vendor
- Microsoft 365 and [backups](/services/backup-disaster-recovery/) that have been restored
- Scanner and tablet standards, including a wipe when seasonal labor leaves
- [Network security](/services/network-security/) for the office and the dock
- [Cybersecurity](/services/cybersecurity/) that assumes the mailbox is still how ransomware arrives
- [Mobile CCTV](/services/mobile-cctv-trailers/) on yards and overflow lots
- On-site from Canoga Park across Los Angeles, the Valley, and the Inland Empire

Projects — a new building, a WMS cutover, a camera package — are quoted separately so the monthly plan stays predictable.

### Yards and cargo theft

Cameras do not replace insurance. They change what happens after close: a live view, a recording, a deterrent on a lot that used to go dark. We already run this layer for [construction](/industries/construction/). A warehouse yard is that problem with a fence and a pickup schedule.

### Coverage and cost

Headquarters is in Canoga Park. On-site across Southern California. Remote for multi-state operators.

Pricing depends on office headcount, buildings, and whether cameras are in scope. We quote after a walkthrough.
