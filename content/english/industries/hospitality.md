## Managed IT for restaurants, hotels, and venues

Secure Techies provides **hospitality IT services** for Southern California restaurants, hotels, and venues that cannot lose a Saturday night to a frozen terminal. From Canoga Park we run [managed help desk](/services/managed-help-desk/), [network security](/services/network-security/), and [cybersecurity](/services/cybersecurity/) around POS, guest Wi-Fi, and the back office.

A hospitality site is two networks pretending to be one: guests on phones, and a payment terminal that has to stay isolated. Shared manager passwords and a consumer router in the office are how those worlds collide. A worked example is [guest Wi-Fi that stopped at the register](/case-studies/restaurant-guest-wifi-isolation/).

### Who we work with

Independent restaurants, small hotel and boutique groups, and venues in Los Angeles, the Valley, and nearby counties. Typical size is one to a handful of locations — enough complexity that informal IT is already failing. Warehouses and distribution sites are a better fit for [warehousing and logistics IT](/industries/warehousing-logistics/).

### What is included

A standard engagement covers:

- [Help desk](/services/managed-help-desk/) during service hours
- POS environment — network, printers, identity. Application support stays with Toast, Square, Oracle, or whoever you run
- Guest, staff, and payment networks on separate paths
- Microsoft 365 for the back office, payroll, and reservations
- [Cybersecurity](/services/cybersecurity/): MFA, email filtering, endpoint protection, same-day revoke when a manager leaves
- [Backup and disaster recovery](/services/backup-disaster-recovery/) for mail and files that hold schedules and vendor records
- Optional [mobile CCTV](/services/mobile-cctv-trailers/) on lots and docks
- On-site from Canoga Park

Projects — a new location, a POS cutover, a network rebuild — are quoted separately so the monthly plan stays predictable.

### Payments and guest Wi-Fi

We do not “certify PCI.” We keep guest traffic off the payment path and write down enough that a QSA can inspect the environment. That is the useful half of PCI for a restaurant group: segmentation, access control, and evidence — not a binder nobody opens.

### Coverage and cost

Headquarters is in Canoga Park. On-site across Los Angeles County and Southern California. Remote for groups with sites outside the region.

Pricing depends on locations, terminals, and whether cameras are in scope. We quote after a free assessment.
